Avaliar is an AI runtime governance platform that monitors, detects, and provides evidence of AI risk in production LLM applications. Whether you are dealing with hallucinations, bias, toxicity, PII leaks, or prompt injection attempts, Avaliar provides real-time detection and actionable insights to keep your AI systems safe and reliable.

## How It Works

Avaliar follows a three-stage lifecycle that takes every LLM interaction from raw data to resolved insight.

<Steps>
  <Step icon="inbox" title="Capture">
    Put the open-source Python SDK guard inside your app, route traffic through the Avaliar Proxy, or send OpenTelemetry data. Every request and response is recorded as a structured trace, and every SDK guard decision as a tamper-evident evidence record.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="magnifying-glass" title="Detect">
    Inputs, outputs, tool calls and tool results are checked for safety issues: prompt injection (covering jailbreaks and SQL injection), toxicity, PII and secrets, hallucination, bias, misinformation, misuse, graphic content, illegal activities, and personal safety. The SDK checks in your process, before anything reaches your model; Proxy and OpenTelemetry traffic is checked on Avaliar's backend.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="triangle-exclamation" title="Alert">
    When a detector finds an issue, Avaliar scores its severity and evaluates it against your alert rules. Notifications are dispatched to your configured channels so your team can investigate and respond immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
